Our favourite places nearby

Benalmádena is lovely all year round – even in winter, daytime temperatures are usually around 17 °C.

400 m

Beaches

The nearest beach, Playa de Santa Ana, is just 400 m away, and there are many more along the coast.

400 m

Seafront promenade

Beach bars, cafés and a long walk by the sea all the way to Torremolinos.

≈ 1 km

Puerto Marina

The marina with restaurants and bars, the Sea Life aquarium and boat trips.

1 km

Parque de la Paloma

A large park with a lake, free-roaming animals and playgrounds – perfect for kids.

≈ 6 km

Castillo de Colomares

A fairy-tale monument to Christopher Columbus on the hillside above the coast.

≈ 6 km

Benalmádena Pueblo & the Stupa

A white Andalusian village above the coast and a Buddhist stupa with views over the whole coastline.

More ideas

  • Cable car up Mount Calamorro – panoramic views over the whole coast.
  • Málaga – about 30 minutes by train: the Alcazaba, the cathedral and the Picasso Museum.
  • Our tip for the evening: Plaza Bonanza with lots of restaurants and bars.
  • Day trips by car: Mijas (25 min), Marbella (35 min), Nerja (1 h), Caminito del Rey (1 h), Ronda (1.5 h), Granada (1 h 45 min).

Good to know

Before you arrive

Check-in

From 15:00. With self check-in you can also arrive later at no extra charge – just let us know when to expect you.

Check-out & minimum stay

Check-out by 10:00. The minimum stay is 4 nights.

Guests

Up to 4 people. Extra beds are not available.

Children

Children of all ages are welcome. A baby cot (0–3 years) is free on request.

Pool

The outdoor pool in the garden of the complex is open from mid-June to the end of September.

Lift

The apartment is on the 8th floor – the second-highest – and the building has a lift.

Parking

No parking space comes with the apartment.

House rules

Non-smoking apartment. No pets, parties, or stag and hen parties.

From the airport

About 15 minutes by taxi, or take the C1 train to Benalmádena–Arroyo de la Miel (approx. 15 min); from there it's 1.4 km.
